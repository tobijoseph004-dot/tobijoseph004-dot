01 / BUSINESS CONTEXT

Questions shaped by
real airline priorities.

As airlines add capacity and expand routes, understanding operational reliability becomes increasingly important. This project explores how public flight data can be used to examine route patterns, delay behaviour and the operational conditions that may influence a passenger’s journey.

The analysis is inspired by publicly communicated priorities in the regional aviation market, including growth, customer experience and reliable operations. It does not assume that any specific problem exists at ValueJet.
